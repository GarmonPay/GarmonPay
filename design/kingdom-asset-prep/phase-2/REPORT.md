# Phase 2 asset reconstruction — incomplete

Date: 2026-10-15

No asset in this folder is approved for the homepage.

The lion reference and the homepage composition reference were not modified. Phase 1 design-system files were not modified. The homepage was not changed.

## What was attempted

Local subject masking lifted the lion’s body, crown, violet eyes, mane, front paws, and gold G coin from the existing pixels. The raised tail was left behind.

A second local pass followed gold and brown pixels from the body into the tail. That pass kept part of the tail curve and also took city windows and a car wheel. That result changes the approved silhouette, so it is rejected.

## Why the remaining pieces were not made

The city behind the lion, the slogan, the button, and the billboard lettering are painted into the same pixels. Those hidden areas are not stored in the file. Filling them to match the approved lighting would be generative inpainting. That was not done.

Because there is no clean lion layer and no clean city plate, these deliverables were not created:

- Approved transparent lion
- Clean city background
- Empty billboard surfaces in the scene
- Desktop composition
- Mobile composition
- Reduced-motion fallback based on reconstructed layers
